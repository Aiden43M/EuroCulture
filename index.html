The Manifesto

Four principles,
set in stone.

  1. I.

    Proportion

    The golden section was not a trend. Harmony is measurable, and people feel it before they can name it.

  2. II.

    Ornament

    Detail is a gift to the passer-by. A carved capital costs once and gives for centuries.

  3. III.

    Permanence

    Build for the grandchildren of strangers. Stone over cladding, craft over cost-per-square-metre.

  4. IV.

    Place

    A street in Paris should not look like a street in Phoenix. Local stone, local style, local pride.

“We shape our buildings;
