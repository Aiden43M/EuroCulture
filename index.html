The Manifesto

Four principles,
set in stone.

  1. I.

    Borders

    A home has a door. Guests are welcome; hosts set the terms. That is not hostility, it is hospitality.

  2. II.

    Faith

    The cathedrals were not built by committees. Europe was raised by believers, and it shows.

  3. III.

    Nations

    Poles, Swiss, Hungarians, Italians. A continent of peoples, not a market of consumers.

  4. IV.

    Beauty

    Proportion, ornament, permanence. Build for the grandchildren of strangers.

“We shape our buildings;
thereafter they shape us.”
— Winston Churchill, 1943
